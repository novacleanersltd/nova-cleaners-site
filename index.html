About Us

Welcome to Nova Cleaners Bradford LTD

At Nova Cleaners Bradford LTD, we believe that a clean space is the absolute foundation for a safe, healthy, and high-quality life. Based right here in Bradford, our mission is to provide reliable, professional cleaning services that elevate standard hygiene into true care—creating environments where people and businesses can truly thrive.

Our Story

Founded in Bradford, Nova Cleaners Bradford LTD was built upon a unique, highly professional combination of hands-on cleaning mastery and extensive experience in care sector management.

Having worked extensively in both professional cleaning and care environments, our founders intimately understand how deeply physical surroundings impact health, mental wellbeing, and peace of mind. We identified an urgent need for a cleaning service that approaches hygiene with the empathy, rigorous safety standards, and obsessive attention to detail typically reserved for healthcare settings.

Our Core Values

Compassion & Understanding: Because of our background in care, we approach every client with empathy, recognizing that an immaculate environment plays a vital role in personal wellbeing.
Uncompromising Quality: We pay meticulous attention to detail, ensuring no corner is missed and every single space meets the highest benchmarks of cleanliness.
Integrity & Safety: We operate with absolute transparency, using safe eco-friendly practices and treating your private or commercial property with the utmost respect.
